# Locked desktop quality target
Approved by the product owner, 7 October 2026.

Build a polished desktop renewable-energy tycoon matching the style, richness and composition of [the user's inspiration images and clips, issue #12](https://github.com/Mr-Meow-ZA/Megawatt-Valley/issues/12): a lush, detailed, stylised **3D isometric** valley where a tiny solar business grows into a lively, thoughtfully organised company.

This is the governing visual and interaction goal for the independent desktop build. Latest user decisions take precedence. Conflicting older pixel-art/browser-first guidance is historical. The current implementation does not set the quality ceiling.

## Required qualities
- Actual stylised 3D with smooth isometric pan/zoom, warm natural colours, coherent materials and grounded shadows.
- Scenic terrain, rivers, banks, cliffs and grouped planting with readable foreground and background.
- Purposeful site composition: roads to entrances, workshop service yards, maintenance aisles and fenced electrical compounds.
- Detailed office, workshop, substation and solar racks: glazing, signage, bays, tools, transformers, insulators, panel cells and visible supports.
- Correct road/fence joins, bends, gates, building frontage, scale and ground contact.
- Visible staff roles, walking, working, cleaning, repairs, breaks and incident reactions; vehicles with routes and destinations.
- A purpose-built management interface: compact resources/objectives, horizontal visual building catalogue and contextual inspectors. Two Point informs character/usability; SimCity informs planning/infrastructure clarity. Earlier HTML layouts impose no constraint.
- Reliable previews, collision rules, repeat placement, road dragging with total cost, selection/removal, right-click/Esc cancellation including mid-drag.
- Honest gameplay descriptions; readable construction, dirt, faults and weather.
- Easy desktop launch, reliable save/close/resume, responsive controls, audio and measured performance.

The intended feeling: “I built this company from almost nothing. This is my place, my people and my operation. I want to see what it becomes next.”

## Scope
First finish **Here Comes the Sun**, roughly 30–60 minutes on first play with optional mastery. Preserve develop/finance/build/operate/improve/expand and manual/staff/policy/automation/exception progression. Building, export, equipment choice, manual repair, Radio Dispatch, cleaning, expansion, capability choices, events, weather preparation and stars remain essential.

Wind/BESS/portfolios shown in references are later progression. Preserve Unity history; do not use Unity or Godot for this independent build. Reference images are design inputs, not licensed production assets.

## Acceptance and recurring review
Compare actual overview, normal and close screenshots against references at similar scales. Review composition, density, silhouettes, lighting, landscape and interface separately. Inspect joins and gates. Capture working staff and vehicles. Exercise placement/cancellation and inspect UI at 1920×1080 and 1366×768. Show construction/dirt/fault/weather states and early-versus-expanded growth. Record performance hardware/resolution/frame times. Retest scenario/jobs/road effects/save-resume as relevant.

Compilation or an isolated attractive screenshot does not prove completion. Maintain an honest gap list. Material changes require a new explicit product decision. On every “proceed”, “resume” or “keep working”, progress toward this target and periodically compare the actual game against it.
